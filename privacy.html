Concert Bot

Privacy Policy

Who handles your information

I’m Matthew Owen Miles. I operate Concert Bot as an individual and decide how the bot uses personal information. You can contact me at mattmiles51@gmail.com.

When you use a command

Discord sends the bot your command and interaction details. The bot uses your Discord user ID and permissions to check who can manage a countdown and operate its private setup menu. The countdown database stores the channel and message IDs, concert name, start time, display settings, last displayed minute and an optional cropped copy of uploaded artwork. Ordinary chat messages are not saved in this database.

Why the bot uses this information

I rely on legitimate interests in providing the countdown a server manager requested, restricting changes to authorised users and diagnosing failures. Discord delivers the commands and displays the poster. Railway hosts the bot, its database and operational logs. Error logs may contain channel IDs and technical details. Anyone with access to the channel can see the published poster and may save a copy.

How long information stays

Setup menus expire after ten minutes of inactivity. The bot keeps the current channel record so it can update the same post. There is no automatic expiry at showtime. Removing the bot or deleting a finished post can leave the record in storage. I can remove the record on request. Operational logs follow the hosting service’s configured retention period.

Change or delete a countdown

A server manager can replace the artwork or run /concert-artwork without an image to clear it. To request deletion of the stored countdown record, email me with the channel ID or message link. You can request removal of your own information or artwork even if you are not a server manager. Discord and other users may retain their own copies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email me to exercise these rights or raise a concern. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
